ABOUT THE OBSERVATORY

Structure, not stories.

Event Horizon is an abstract map of connections between notes. Sectors are numbered, nodes use anonymous IDs, and no titles, text, paths, or semantic labels are displayed.

Topology is not guaranteed anonymity. A distinctive pattern of connections can fingerprint a graph, even without its content.

Colored stars are actual sampled notes spiraling inward through 3D space. Each sector returns to its outer orbit on a repeating visual loop: no notes or connections are deleted. The paths and black hole are artistic, not a physical simulation. The turbulent gas and lensed background sky are artwork, not extra notes. Short colored trails follow actual note positions and clear when a sector recycles. Only real within-sector connections are drawn, with endpoints following the moving notes.

In Orbit, drag to orbit and scroll or pinch to zoom. Select a sector to isolate and follow it. In Fly, use WASD to move, Q/E for down/up, and drag to look; touch users can hold the six thrust buttons. Selecting a sector returns to tracking Orbit. Reset view restores the home camera. Cinematic view hides the HUD; its persistent exit button or Escape restores it. Render quality separately offers Mobile, Desktop and Cinematic budgets. Escape otherwise clears a selection. Pause and reduced motion freeze stars, trails and gas; deliberate camera controls still work.
