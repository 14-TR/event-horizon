ABOUT THE OBSERVATORY

Structure, not stories.

Event Horizon is an abstract map of connections between notes. Sectors are numbered, nodes use anonymous IDs, and no titles, text, paths, or semantic labels are displayed.

Topology is not guaranteed anonymity. A distinctive pattern of connections can fingerprint a graph, even without its content.

Positions and the black hole are artistic, not a physical simulation. The black hole, dust, and background stars are decoration; colored sector stars represent notes. Only real within-sector connections are drawn. A bounded sample keeps the observatory responsive.

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, and select a sector to isolate it. Use sector buttons and the node selector for keyboard exploration. Escape clears a selection. Motion respects your system’s reduced-motion preference.
